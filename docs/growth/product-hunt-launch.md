# Step 3 — Product Hunt Launch Kit

A Product Hunt launch gives you two things at once: a **one-day traffic spike**
and a **permanent high-authority backlink** (plus auto-generated "alternative to"
pages). Everything you need to launch is below — copy, paste, tweak.

## Before launch (1–2 weeks out)

- [ ] Create/complete your PH profile and add a real photo — new accounts with no history get throttled.
- [ ] Follow ~20–30 active hunters/makers in the design & productivity space; engage genuinely so you're not a stranger on launch day.
- [ ] Prepare assets (see checklist below).
- [ ] Line up 10–20 people (friends, early users, communities) who'll genuinely try it and comment on launch day. **Never ask for upvotes directly** — PH penalises it. Ask them to "check it out and leave honest feedback."
- [ ] Pick the launch day: **Tuesday–Thursday**, and schedule for **12:01 AM PT** (PH resets at midnight Pacific — launching early gives you the full day to accumulate).

## Maker replies (keep these handy for common questions)

**"How is this free / what's the catch?"**
> No catch on the core tools — they run in your browser, so they cost me almost
> nothing to serve. A few heavier AI features have a generous free tier, but
> background removal, compression, conversion, crop and the rest are just free.

**"Is my image uploaded anywhere?"**
> For most tools, no — the processing happens locally in your browser, so the
> image never leaves your device. Where a tool does need the server, the image is
> only used to produce your result and isn't stored or shared.

**"How is it different from remove.bg / Canva / TinyPNG?"**
> Mainly: no watermark and no account required, and it's all in one place instead
> of one tool per site. There's a side-by-side on each of these here:
> https://www.sjpt.io/alternatives

**"Feature request: X"**
> Love it — adding to the list. Thanks for trying it! 🙏 (Then actually build the popular ones.)

## Asset checklist

- [ ] **Logo / thumbnail:** 240×240 px, PNG, clean on light and dark.
- [ ] **Gallery images:** 1270×760 px (or 1.67:1). Aim for 4–6:
  1. Hero — the toolkit landing / tool grid.
  2. Background remover before/after.
  3. Upscaler before/after (zoom crop showing detail).
  4. Compressor showing "X MB → Y KB, no visible quality loss".
  5. The "no watermark / no sign-up" promise as a clean graphic.
  6. The `/alternatives` comparison table.
- [ ] **Demo video/GIF (optional but boosts conversion):** 30–60s screen recording of removing a background and downloading — no account, no watermark.
- [ ] Make sure every screenshot shows the **sjpt.io** wordmark somewhere.

## Launch-day timeline (Pacific time)

| Time | Do |
|------|----|
| 12:01 AM | Publish. Post your first comment immediately. |
| Morning | Share to your networks with "we're live on PH" (link to the PH page, not the site). Post in relevant communities where self-promo is allowed. |
| All day | Reply to **every** comment within minutes. Engagement ranking rewards active makers. |
| Midday | Email your waitlist/early users asking for honest feedback on PH. |
| Evening | Thank everyone; recap what you learned; note top feature requests publicly. |

## After launch

- Add a "🏆 on Product Hunt" embed/badge to your site footer (links back, social proof).
- PH auto-creates **alternative-to** pages — these are extra backlinks; make sure your alternatives are listed.
- Repurpose the launch comment into a short blog post / LinkedIn post.
- Whatever the ranking, you keep the backlink and the feedback — that's the real win.

## Don'ts (these get launches penalised)

- ❌ Don't ask for upvotes anywhere, ever. Ask for *feedback*.
- ❌ Don't use vote rings / incentivised upvotes.
- ❌ Don't launch and disappear — un-answered comments kill momentum.
- ❌ Don't relaunch the identical product repeatedly; save relaunches for a genuinely new major feature.
